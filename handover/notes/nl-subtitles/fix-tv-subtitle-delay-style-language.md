## Line
- **M5 Subtitles** (merged): search and download through Jellyfin's OpenSubtitles plugin, appearance (size, background, position) and delay, on the web and the TV. Text subtitle URLs always end in `Stream.vtt` (Jellyfin converts; the TV app only reads WebVTT, so `.srt` DeliveryUrls showed nothing). TV: subtitle menus and the finder pause the film while open; "off" hides Media3's subtitle view instead of re-selecting tracks; text tracks are fetched once 8 s after a stream starts so Jellyfin has them extracted. The finder is a right-hand sheet on the TV (cleaned release names, one plain meta line, no Close button: Back closes). **TV bridge v7 (2026-10-27, fixes from a real-TV evening; not yet run on the TV):** (1) Delay: `setSubtitleDelay(seconds)`; Media3 1.5.1 has no offset, so `DelayedTextRenderer` (in `SubtitleLook.kt`, added by `NlRenderersFactory`) asks the text renderer for the subtitles of a shifted time; per stream (play() resets it, the page sends it again). (2) Look: Media3's view only styles plain text lines, so disc picture subtitles (PGS) and ASS lines ignored size, background and position. `SubtitleLook` rewrites the cues: picture subtitles are scaled around their bottom centre, raised, and drawn on a dark box when Background is Box; text lines with their own position are raised when in the lower half. (3) Language off by one: an in-file track was picked only by its position among the file's subtitles, so when Media3 left one out the next track showed (Italian gave Hindi). `embeddedTextGroupPosition` now checks the language too and picks the same-language track by rank if the position lands on another language.
